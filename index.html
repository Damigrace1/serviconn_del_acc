Request Account Deletion – ServiConn

We're Sad to See You Go 😞

Your privacy and control matter to us. You can delete your ServiConn account and associated data at any time using the process outlined below.

🛠️ How to Delete Your Account

  • Open the ServiConn app.
  • Go to Profile → Settings.
  • Select "Delete My Account" and confirm your request.
  • Or email us directly at support@serviconn.com.

🧹 What Will Be Deleted?

  • Your full profile (name, photo, contact info, preferences).
  • Your listed services, booking history, and referral data.
  • All associated personal data stored with your account.

📁 What We May Retain

  • Transaction and payment records (as required by law).
  • Anonymous app usage analytics for product improvement.
⚠️ Important: This action is permanent. Once deleted, your data and services cannot be recovered.
